About Us

EmpowerHer is a technology-based community focused on training and promoting women in the field of information technology. We aim to spread awareness of the opportunities available and to include as many women as possible.

The community was conceived by the founders of Midtown Tech Hub, and an initial programme to promote women was held at the hub. EmpowerHer was officially launched in 2021 with dedicated volunteers working to grow the community.

Today we are a growing community of members and volunteers working together behind the scenes to ensure smooth operations.

Our Goals

As a community centred around empowering women, some of our goals include:

Challenge & Connect

EmpowerHer helps women grow by challenging them with assignments that require learning new skills, while connecting them with mentors who provide feedback and support.

Build an Inclusive Culture

We aim to set aside unconscious bias about the capabilities of women by encouraging a review of diversity and inclusion initiatives, so that equity receives the attention it deserves.

Invest

We invest in women by offering formal leadership development opportunities focused on building the competencies needed to thrive in the technology space.

Meet the Founder

Elizabeth Okaome is the founder of the EmpowerHer community. A graduate of Microbiology from the University of Benin, she combines intellect with ambition, integrity and vision.

"It took me a while to get here, but after discovering the gap between men and women in the field of technology, I knew I had to do my part. I had the idea of creating a community like this while in my second year at the University of Benin — a place where women don't have to worry about being themselves. When I am around some people, they are often surprised to learn that I am three things: young, a CEO, and not a man." 

              Elizabeth Okaome
