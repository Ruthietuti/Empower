Introduction

EmpowerHer is a technology community focused on raising and promoting women in tech. The community was initiated by Elizabeth Okaome, a co-founder of Midtown Tech Hub.

We organise bootcamps and training programmes to help women build practical skills in the field of technology.

The Different Tracks

Data Science

Learn how to work with data — from analysis and visualisation to deriving insights that drive real decisions. This track builds the foundations you need to start a career in data.

Digital Marketing

Discover how brands reach and engage audiences online. This track covers the essentials of digital marketing so you can grow your skills and your confidence in a fast-moving field.

Graphic Design

Develop your creative eye and learn the principles of visual communication. This track helps you turn ideas into clear, professional designs.

3D Printing

Explore how digital models become real objects. This hands-on track introduces the tools and workflows behind 3D design and printing.

Web Design

Learn to design and build modern websites from the ground up. This track covers the essential skills for becoming a successful web developer, along with the career opportunities that come with them. Sign up and get the full experience.
