What We Stand For

Our Vision

Our vision is a community where information technology is seen as accessible to every woman. We provide opportunities for women to enter the industry and make a smooth transition, regardless of their previous background.

We aim to train over five thousand women in information technology and support more than seventy percent of them into employment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make the field of information technology accessible to women from all walks of life. We champion inclusion for women regardless of age, financial status, income bracket or background.

We empower women to pursue and live purpose-driven lives by making the field of information technology open to all women.

empowerher©

EmpowerHer is a technology-focused community dedicated to training and promoting women in the field of information technology. We aim to spread awareness of the opportunities available and to include as many women as possible.
